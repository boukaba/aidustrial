# Milking and milk quality (milking)

## Throughput
Every minute a cow waits past her slot reduces the milk let-down for that
milking, and repeated delay costs yield across the whole lactation rather than
the day. Parlour downtime is therefore measured in minutes per 24 hours.

## Bulk tank
Milk must reach the tank below 4 C. Condenser fouling raises the tank
temperature slowly; by the time it is obvious the load may already be out of
specification. Clean the condenser on condition, not on schedule.

## Somatic cell count
Bulk somatic cell count above 200,000 cells/ml indicates subclinical mastitis
circulating in the herd. Above 400,000 the milk may be rejected outright.

## Culling
Culling a chronic case is permanent. It is always a human decision, and it is
the clearest example of why the gate exists.
