# Fodder crops and irrigation (crop)

## Soil moisture
Wilting point for the sandy Saharan soils is around 12 percent volumetric.
Below that, growth stops regardless of how much water is applied afterwards.

## Irrigation timing
Irrigating a field that is already at or above field capacity wastes water and
leaches nutrients. Check moisture before starting a pivot, not after.

## Harvest
Harvest when dry matter is at the target for the crop. Cutting early gives
higher quality but lower tonnage; cutting late gives the reverse. The right
answer depends on the current feed cover.

## Salinity
Repeated irrigation with high-conductivity water accumulates salt in the root
zone. Yield decline from salinity is gradual and easy to attribute to something
else.
