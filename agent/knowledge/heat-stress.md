# Heat stress protocol (veterinarian)

## Thresholds
Heat stress begins when the temperature-humidity index reaches 72. At 78 it is
moderate; above 82 it is severe. In Adrar summer conditions the index crosses
72 by mid-morning and 82 in the early afternoon.

## Rumination as an early signal
Rumination drops 20 to 25 percent before overt heat stress is visible. A chewing
time below 0.7 of the group baseline is treated as an early warning even when
the index is still moderate. Yield loss follows rumination loss by roughly one
day, so acting on rumination is the only way to act before the loss is banked.

## Cooling priority
Where misting hardware is not available in every barn, cool the groups with the
highest index exposure first, then the highest yield. Never split cooling across
all groups equally: partial cooling everywhere produces less benefit than full
cooling where the load is highest.

## Escalation
Shedding load is a last resort. It reduces throughput for the whole lactation,
not just the day, so it requires a human decision.
