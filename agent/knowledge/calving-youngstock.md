# Calving and youngstock (calving)

## The first calving
Heifers should reach target breeding weight by 23 months. Below 79 percent of
target, first calving slips and the animal produces nothing for longer.

## Colostrum
Calves need colostrum within the first six hours. Beyond that, absorption of
immunoglobulins falls sharply and the calf is exposed for the rest of its life.

## Scours
Scours in a calf group spreads within hours. Isolation of affected animals is
cheap and reversible; waiting for a confirmed diagnosis is not.

## Growth
Daily gain below 0.7 kg/day in heifers delays first calving by months, which is
a cost carried for the whole life of the animal.
