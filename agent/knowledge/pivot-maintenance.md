# Pivot maintenance (maintenance)

## Vibration
Bearing vibration above 7.1 mm/s is the warning band. Above 11.2 mm/s the unit
should be stopped rather than run to failure. A bearing that seizes in the field
strands the pivot mid-irrigation and the field loses the pass.

## Temperature
Bearing housing temperature above 70 C indicates lubrication breakdown. Ambient
in Adrar is high, so the margin between normal and alarm is narrower than in
temperate sites; compare against the ambient-adjusted target, not a fixed number.

## Nozzles
Blocked nozzles create uneven application, which shows up as a dry arc in the
field rather than an alarm. Count them on inspection. More than four blocked on
one unit means the application pattern is no longer uniform.

## Work orders
Routine servicing and inspection can be dispatched by the system without
approval: sending a technician to look at something changes nothing physical.
Stopping or restarting a pivot is a human decision.
