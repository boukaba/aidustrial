# Water and the aquifer (agronomist)

## The constraint
Irrigation draws from the Albien, a fossil aquifer with no meaningful recharge.
Every cubic metre used is removed permanently. The site budget is expressed as a
projected annual draw against a permitted envelope, not as a monthly bill.

## Quantity
Track draw against the envelope continuously. A pivot left running after harvest
is the single most common avoidable loss. Restarting a pivot is always a human
decision because it commits water that cannot be returned.

## Quality
Water quality matters as much as volume. Conductivity above 3.0 dS/m stresses
fodder crops and reduces germination. Sodium adsorption ratio above 9 degrades
soil structure over a season, which is irreversible within the programme.

## Interaction with fodder
A field that is drying is not only a crop problem. Fodder shortfall propagates
to herd rations within days, and to milk yield within a week.
